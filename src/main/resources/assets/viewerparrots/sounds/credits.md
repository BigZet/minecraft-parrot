# Real parrot recordings

The five clips below were selected by the user from the audio preview on
14 October 2026 (preview numbers 01, 12, 14, 15 and 27). The packaged Ogg files
are byte-for-byte copies of those prepared preview clips; no further editing
or re-encoding was performed for inclusion in the mod.

## Preparation of the preview clips

Each original recording was trimmed to the interval listed above, converted
to mono at 48 kHz, peak-normalized to a maximum of −6 dB, given 5 ms fades at
the edges and encoded as Ogg Vorbis. Pitch and playback speed were unchanged.
These are recordings of real parrots, not synthesized or Minecraft mob sounds.

Source metadata and excerpt timings are preserved from
`artifacts/parrot-audio-selection/manifest.json` in the working project.
